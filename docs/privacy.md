# pikapik Privacy Policy

pikapik has no developer-run analytics or tracking.

- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account.
- Your settings and Pet progress stay on your Mac. They are never sent anywhere.
- Keyboard, mouse and window events are handled in memory to run the feature you turned on. They are never stored or sent.
- If you turn on App Volume, the sound of an app whose volume you change passes through pikapik on your Mac while it plays. It is processed on the fly, never recorded or saved, and never sent anywhere. The microphone is never used.
- Brightness levels you set for your monitors are stored only on this Mac. pikapik talks to a monitor only over its display cable and sends nothing anywhere.
- In the App Store version, Speed Test contacts speed.cloudflare.com only when you start a check. It downloads test data and uploads generated bytes, not your files. Cloudflare receives your connection IP address and processes network information under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Its [Speed Test privacy explanation](https://speed.cloudflare.com/) describes estimated city/country, ISP information and anonymized measurement sharing. An ephemeral session does not prevent server-side processing; this app does not control Cloudflare's retention.
- Optional tips use Apple's StoreKit to load available products and process a purchase when you choose one. Tips do not unlock features and no subscription is required.
